First, look at the top right corner of her license

With no passport, Gloria's Connecticut license decides whether she clears airport security. What matters is whether it has a gold star, which marks it as a REAL ID. The renewal due next week matters much less.

★It has a gold star

She can fly on it this week
  • TSA accepts a REAL ID license for up to two years after it expires, so next week's expiration date doesn't affect this trip.
  • Renew online before the expiration date to avoid Connecticut's $25 late fee. It takes about seven minutes on the CT DMV site and works from anywhere, including Florida.
  • Pick the two-year renewal offered to drivers 65 and older. It costs $24, compared with $72 or $96 for a longer license.
  • The new card is mailed within 30 days to the address DMV has on file. She keeps her current card, which is the one she'll fly with, so ask someone at home to watch the mail.
  • Online renewal isn't available if her last renewal didn't include a new photo. In that case, renew by mail from Florida with Form B-350, sent at least 10 days before expiration, or in person once she's back.

No star, or it says "Not for Federal Identification"

Plan on TSA ConfirmID
  • TSA stopped accepting licenses without the star on May 7, 2025.
  • Fastest fix this week: pay the $45 TSA ConfirmID fee on Pay.gov before leaving for the airport. Enter her legal name and September 24 as the travel date. It covers 10 days. Print or screenshot the receipt; TSA needs to see it.
  • TSA then tries to confirm her identity at the checkpoint. Success isn't guaranteed and it can add about 30 minutes, so get to Bradley at least 2½ hours early with her license and any other ID that shows her name.
  • A REAL ID needs an in-person DMV appointment with her U.S. birth certificate, Social Security card, and two pieces of mail dated within 90 days. The card arrives in about 20 business days, which is too late for Thursday but worth doing before she flies home.
  • If she flies home more than 10 days after September 24 without a REAL ID, the $45 fee applies again. TSA doesn't accept paper temporary licenses.

Bereavement and senior fares: what actually exists

No airline combines a senior discount with a bereavement discount. Only a few airlines still have bereavement programs, and today they mostly offer a more flexible ticket, not a lower price.

Delta

Worth one phone call

Delta covers travel for a family member's imminent death, such as a sister in hospice, through its Medical Emergency Fare program. She needs a free SkyMiles account, and the booking is done by phone at 800-221-1212. Have the hospice's name and phone number and the attending doctor's name ready. Travel has to start within seven days.

Compare the quote against the $378.78 Delta fare found online. Delta says its regular fares can be lower.

Alaska Airlines

Doesn't fit this route

Takes 10% off the lowest fare for travel within seven days of a death. No Alaska flights appeared between Bradley and Florida in these searches.

American, Southwest, United, Breeze, Frontier

No bereavement fare

American and Southwest both state they don't offer one, and United has no dedicated program. Breeze and Frontier don't publish one. Their regular low fares, like the Breeze flight above, are already the cheapest route.

Senior fares, 65 and older

Rare, but free to check

American, United and Delta still offer senior fares on a few routes. On aa.com or united.com, change the traveler type to "Senior (65+)" and compare. Southwest ended senior fares in 2019, and Frontier's 55-and-over offer ended in February 2025. None of these is likely to beat about $235.

Only call phone numbers printed on the airline's own website. Search results are full of look-alike "bereavement" numbers run by third-party booking agents.

What these numbers can and can't tell you

  • How the totals were built. Kiwi.com was searched with one checked bag included in its price. Expedia and lastminute.com show the fare alone, so the airline's first-bag fee from the Expedia listing was added. Totals marked ≈ use an estimated bag price.
